## About this trip

Shantiniketan, the "abode of peace", is where Tagore founded his university with classes held under the trees. Today it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site full of art, music and red-earth landscapes.

## Itinerary

- 6:00 AM pickup from Kolkata
- Visva-Bharati campus, Uttarayan complex and Tagore museum
- Lunch with authentic Bengali thali
- Sonajhuri haat (handicraft market) with Baul singers *(Saturdays)*
- Return to Kolkata by 9 PM

## Good to know

- Visva-Bharati museum is closed on Wednesdays — we plan around it
- Best on a Saturday for the haat
