## About this trip

Mayapur is the birthplace of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu and the world headquarters of ISKCON. Across the river, Nabadwip has been a centre of Sanskrit learning and devotion for centuries.

## Itinerary

- 6:30 AM pickup from Kolkata
- ISKCON Mayapur campus and the Temple of the Vedic Planetarium
- Prasad lunch
- Ferry across the Ganga to Nabadwip, walk through the old town
- Evening aarti, then return to Kolkata by 10 PM
