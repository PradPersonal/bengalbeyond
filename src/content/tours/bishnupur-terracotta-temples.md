## About this trip

The Malla kings of Bishnupur built dozens of temples covered in intricately carved terracotta panels — scenes from the Ramayana, Mahabharata and everyday life, still crisp after 400 years.

## Itinerary

- 5:30 AM pickup from Kolkata
- Rasmancha, Jor Bangla and Shyam Rai temples
- Lunch
- Visit a Baluchari silk weaving workshop and terracotta artisans' village
- Return to Kolkata by 9 PM

## Good to know

- A great trip for photographers — carry a wide lens
- Baluchari sarees and terracotta horses make wonderful souvenirs
