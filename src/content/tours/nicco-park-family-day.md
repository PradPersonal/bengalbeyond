## About this tour

**Nicco Park** in Salt Lake has been Kolkata's favourite amusement park for decades. It has rides for both thrill-seekers and young children. We handle the tickets and transport so you can just enjoy the day.

## Itinerary

1. Pickup from your hotel at about 10:30 AM
2. Collect tickets and a quick orientation from your escort
3. Rides and attractions at your own pace
4. Lunch at the park food court (paid separately)
5. Drop back at your hotel by about 4:30 PM

## Good to know

- Bring a change of clothes if you plan to try the water rides
- Some rides have height or age limits
- Weekdays are much less crowded than weekends and holidays
