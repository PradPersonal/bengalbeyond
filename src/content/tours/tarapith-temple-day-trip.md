## About this trip

**Tarapith** is a small temple town near Rampurhat, and its temple to **Maa Tara** draws devotees from across India. The town is closely linked with the 19th-century saint **Bamakhepa**, and its riverside cremation ground (*mahashmashan*) is an important site for Tantric practice.

## Itinerary

- 4:30 AM pickup from Kolkata
- Breakfast stop on the way
- Darshan at the Maa Tara temple (our guide helps you through the queue)
- Bamakhepa's samadhi and the mahashmashan by the Dwarka river
- Lunch, then a short rest
- Return to Kolkata by about 9 PM

## Good to know

- It is a long day, about 5 hours each way by road
- Weekends, new-moon days and festival days are very crowded. Weekdays are calmer
- Prefer to stay overnight? Ask us about a 2-day version with a hotel in Tarapith
