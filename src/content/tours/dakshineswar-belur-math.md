## About this tour

The **Dakshineswar Kali Temple** was built in 1855 by Rani Rashmoni on the east bank of the Hooghly. Sri Ramakrishna lived and taught here, and it is one of the most visited temples in Bengal. Across the river stands **Belur Math**, founded by Swami Vivekananda, whose main temple blends Hindu, Islamic and Christian architecture.

## Itinerary

1. **6:30 AM pickup** from your hotel, to reach before the crowds
2. Darshan at the Kali temple, the twelve Shiva temples and the Radha-Krishna temple
3. Sri Ramakrishna's room and the Panchavati grove
4. Ferry across the Ganga to Belur Math
5. Belur Math temple and riverside grounds
6. Drop back at your hotel by about 11:30 AM

## Good to know

- Dress modestly (shoulders and knees covered); shoes are left outside the temples
- Phones and cameras are not allowed inside the Dakshineswar temple. A cloak room is available
- Both temples close for a few hours in the middle of the day, so this is a morning tour
