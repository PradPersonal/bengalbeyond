## About this tour

**Eco Park (Prakriti Tirtha)** in New Town is one of the largest urban parks in India, built around a big lake with an island in the middle. It is a favourite with families and makes an easy, green break from the busy city.

## Itinerary

1. Pickup from your hotel at about 2:00 PM
2. Walk or cycle through the themed gardens
3. Photos at the Seven Wonders replicas
4. Boating on the lake
5. Optional visit to Mother's Wax Museum nearby
6. Evening snacks, then drop back at your hotel

## Good to know

- Good for families with children and older travellers. Battery cars are available inside the park
- Eco Park is usually closed on Mondays
- Boating, cycling and the wax museum are paid separately at the counter
- Easy to combine with the Biswa Bangla Gate evening tour
