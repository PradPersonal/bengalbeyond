## About this tour

Kolkata was the capital of British India until 1911, and the old business district around **BBD Bagh (Dalhousie Square)** still feels like a living museum. This relaxed walk tells the stories behind the buildings — the merchants, rebels and poets who shaped the city.

## Itinerary

1. **Meet at Esplanade Metro (Gate 1)** — 7:00 AM, before the city gets busy
2. Writers' Building, Lal Dighi and the General Post Office
3. St. John's Church and Job Charnock's mausoleum
4. Morning *bhaar* (clay cup) tea at a century-old stall
5. Finish at **Prinsep Ghat** by the river

## Good to know

- Wear comfortable shoes — about 3 km of walking at an easy pace
- Evening version available on request (ends with sunset at Prinsep Ghat)
