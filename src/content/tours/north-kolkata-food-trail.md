## About this tour

Kolkata eats with its heart. On this trail we skip the tourist restaurants and go where locals have queued for generations — tiny shops that have been frying the same recipe for a hundred years.

## What you'll taste

- Kochuri with aloo torkari for breakfast
- Telebhaja (Bengali fritters) and jhalmuri
- Kabiraji cutlet
- Rosogolla, sandesh and mishti doi straight from the source
- Coffee at the iconic **Indian Coffee House**
