## About this trip

The Sundarbans is a UNESCO World Heritage Site where rivers meet the Bay of Bengal. You'll spend most of your time on a boat, slipping through narrow creeks and watching for crocodiles, kingfishers, spotted deer — and if you're lucky, a tiger.

## Itinerary

**Day 1** — Pickup from Kolkata at 6:30 AM → drive to Godkhali jetty → board the boat, lunch on board → afternoon cruise → evening at the eco-lodge with Bonbibi folk performance.

**Day 2** — Early-morning cruise to Sajnekhali and Sudhanyakhali watchtowers → lunch on board → return to Kolkata by 8 PM.

## Good to know

- Carry a valid ID (needed for forest permits)
- Best season: October to March
