## About this tour

**Biswa Bangla Gate** (also called Kolkata Gate) is a giant ring that spans a main road in New Town. Its top level has a viewing gallery and a restaurant, and at sunset you can watch the lights come on across the city below.

## Itinerary

1. Pickup from your hotel at about 4:30 PM
2. Go up to the ring before sunset and take in the views
3. Optional tea or dinner at the restaurant on top
4. Night drive through New Town, past the Biswa Bangla Convention Centre
5. Drop back at your hotel by about 7:30 PM

## Good to know

- Sunset times change through the year, so we adjust the pickup time to match
- Food at the top restaurant is paid separately
- Easy to combine with the Eco Park day out, which is nearby
